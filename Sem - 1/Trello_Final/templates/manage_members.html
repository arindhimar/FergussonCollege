Understanding Member Roles

  • Owner Full control over the board, including deleting it and managing all members.
  • Admin Can manage board settings and members, but cannot delete the board.
  • Editor Can create, edit, and delete tasks, but cannot change board settings or manage members.
  • Viewer Can only view the board and its tasks, but cannot make any changes.
