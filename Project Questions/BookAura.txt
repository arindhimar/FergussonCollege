Absolutely! Here's a breakdown of **easy**, **medium**, and **hard** interview questions\*\* based on your **Swar Setu – Bridge of Words** project:

---

### ✅ EASY QUESTIONS

These test your understanding of what you built and why:

1. **What is Swar Setu and what problem does it solve?**
2. **What technologies did you use in this project?**
3. **How do users interact with the platform?**
4. **What are the different user roles in Swar Setu?**
5. **How do users upload books?**
6. **Which languages are supported for translation and TTS?**
7. **What is the purpose of the Bhashini API in your project?**
8. **Can users read and listen to the books?**
9. **What is the role of the publisher in your system?**
10. **How long did it take to complete the project?**

---

### 🧠 MEDIUM QUESTIONS

These require understanding of backend/frontend architecture, feature design, and technical challenges:

1. **How does the book translation and audio generation pipeline work?**
2. **How did you integrate the Bhashini API for translation and TTS?**
3. **How did you implement voice command support in the UI?**
4. **What database structure did you design for storing books and their categories?**
5. **How does the role-based access control (RBAC) work in your app?**
6. **What steps did you take to make the UI accessible for visually impaired users?**
7. **How are uploaded files (PDFs, covers, audios) stored and retrieved?**
8. **What happens if a translation or TTS conversion fails?**
9. **How did you handle secure authentication in your platform?**
10. **How did your team divide the work during development?**

---

### 🔥 HARD QUESTIONS

These test your ability to solve edge cases, think about scalability, and handle real-world deployment:

1. **How would you scale Swar Setu to handle thousands of users and files?**
2. **How would you make TTS more natural, especially for regional accents?**
3. **If the Bhashini API becomes unavailable, how would you handle fallback gracefully?**
4. **How would you make the platform work offline for remote schools with limited internet?**
5. **How would you test accessibility features for non-visual users effectively?**
6. **What would you change in your architecture if you were to make Swar Setu a SaaS platform for NGOs?**
7. **How would you handle copyright and moderation issues for user-uploaded books?**
8. **How can AI improve book recommendations for visually impaired readers?**
9. **How would you optimize PDF-to-audio generation for large books or slow devices?**
10. **What are some ethical considerations when handling content for vulnerable users like blind students?**
